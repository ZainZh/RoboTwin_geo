\section{Conclusion}
We presented an object-centric continuous semantic field that separates the points used to observe an object from the locations where policy-facing semantics are read. Conditioned on a partial object point cloud, the field produces part-aware embeddings at resampled query locations and exports them as a semantic point cloud to a DP3-based policy without changing its action objective. Part anchoring and cross-instance alignment organize the field around functional correspondences, while consistency regularization encourages reduced sensitivity to changes in the support observation. Across four RoboTwin tasks and four real-world bimanual tasks, the resulting representation improves manipulation success over raw point clouds, lifted 2D features, and point-wise 3D features, with larger margins over these baselines in the real-world evaluation. Ablations further show that part anchoring and cross-instance alignment provide the dominant training signals, while support/query separation contributes an additional architectural benefit. These results support object-conditioned semantic readout as a reliable interface for robust manipulation under observation and instance variation.
